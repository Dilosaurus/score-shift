# Bands, sign-in and the catalog

Written 2026-09-11 when ScoreShift moved from invite-code libraries to signed-in bands.

## The model

- **A person signs in** with Google or a passwordless email link (Firebase Auth, enabled in the console the same day). Membership follows the person, not the device.
- **A band** is the shared space a group plays from. It owns charts, books and sets. Everyone in the band sees the same charts, books, sets and keys. A person can belong to several bands and switches between them at the top of the Library sheet.
- **Joining** is by invite link (`/?join=CODE`). Opening it adds the signed-in person to the band. Nothing merges: a person's other bands are untouched. The owner can mint a new link (older links stop working) and remove members. Members can leave.
- **The catalog** is what the app ships: The Real Book, The Colorado Cookbook and Golden Lady, served as static files under `dist/samples/` and listed by `dist/transcriptions.mjs` (generated by the transcription coordinator) plus `dist/catalog.mjs`. A band never receives a copy. Tapping a catalog chart adds a **reference** to the band (a score document with `catalog` set and no blobs) and the reader fetches the MXL and PDF from hosting. Editing the notes forks the chart: the band then stores its own MusicXML blob and `userEdited` is set.

The transcription queue page (`/real-book.html`) is no longer linked from the app. It still works at its own URL, and `?score=ID` and `?review=ID` still open a catalog chart or a review copy for QA.

## Firestore layout

```
users/{uid}                  name, email, photo, bands: {bandId: {name, joinedAt}}, currentBand
bands/{id}                   name, owner (uid), invite (current code), createdAt, updatedAt
bands/{id}/members/{uid}     name, role (owner|member), joinedAt, invite (code used to join)
bands/{id}/scores/{scoreId}  chart metadata (+ blobs/{field}.{index} chunks unless it is a catalog reference)
bands/{id}/lists/{listId}    books and sets, unchanged shape
invites/{code}               band, createdBy, createdAt   (26-character code; the code is the secret)
libraries/{code}/...         the pre-band invite-code libraries, kept for claiming and for the chart publisher
```

`firestore.rules` enforces: a band's data is readable and writable only by its members (a member document must exist); a member document can be created by the owner right after creating the band, or by anyone presenting an invite code that resolves to that band; only the owner updates the band document, mints or retires invites, and removes members.

## Code

- `dist/auth.mjs` sign-in (Google popup with redirect fallback, email link send and complete).
- `dist/bands.mjs` the directory: create, join, leave, rename, rotate invite, watch members, and `claimLibrary` which brings an old invite-code library into a new band (catalog copies become references, imported charts are copied with their blobs, books and sets keep their ids).
- `dist/catalog.mjs` the catalog list, book grouping, asset URLs and reference documents. Reference ids keep the old `visual-<id>` and `golden-lady-full-v1` ids so books and sets written before bands still resolve.
- `dist/songbook.mjs` the sync layer, now rooted at `bands/{id}` (or a legacy library for claiming). A reference syncs metadata only (`syncedBlobFields`).
- `dist/app.mjs` the sign-in and first-band screens (`#welcome-dialog`), the band section of the Library sheet, the Catalog tab, and band switching. The device cache in IndexedDB is scoped by band.

Tests: `tests/bands.test.mjs`, `tests/catalog.test.mjs`, `tests/auth.test.mjs`, plus the reference case in `tests/songbook.test.mjs`.

## Migration for an existing library

On first sign-in, a device that holds an old library code (localStorage, or a `?library=CODE` link) is offered **Claim as band** on the first-band screen. The claim copies everything into a new band the person owns. The old library is left in place; the device remembers the claim so it is not offered twice.

## For the transcription coordinator (Astra)

`tools/publish-charts.mjs` still pushes approved charts into the legacy library named in `library.local.json`. Bands do not read that library; they read the catalog straight from `dist/transcriptions.mjs` and `dist/samples/`, so a deploy is what makes a newly approved chart appear in the Catalog tab. The publisher can be retired whenever convenient; nothing depends on it now.

## Guest mode: browsing without an account (2026-09-11)

Nobody has to sign in to use the reader. The sign-in screen carries a door, **Continue without an account**, and a person who takes it plays from this device only:

- The Library head reads **Not signed in** with a dashed empty seat for an avatar; the status line says *Only on this device*. Tapping the head, or **Sign in** on the account line, opens the same sign-in screen, now closable (**Not now**).
- The catalog works in full: tap to preview, **Keep on this device** from a chart's menu or the Chart sheet. Imports, binders, setlists, keys, playback and export all work. Everything is stored in the device cache (IndexedDB) under the `device` scope (`GUEST_BAND` in `dist/guest.mjs`); Firestore is never touched.
- The choice is remembered (`scoreshift-guest` in localStorage): the next launch goes straight in as a guest. A sign-in email link or a band invite link (`?join=`) still shows the sign-in screen, because the link is why the person is there; an invite hides the guest door, since joining needs an account.
- **Signing in later never merges anything by itself.** After sign-in, a box at the top of the Library (*On this device*) says how many charts and lists from before are still on the device and offers **Bring them in** (into the current band; a catalog reference the band already holds is skipped, and list items keep pointing at it because the ids match) or **Forget them** (two taps). Until one is chosen the box stays, and the charts stay on the device.

`bootMode()` and `planBringIn()` in `dist/guest.mjs` decide the boot path and the bring-in plan; `tests/guest.test.mjs` covers them. In `dist/app.mjs`: `askSignIn` (the screen, three outcomes), `enterGuest`, `enterSignedIn`, `signInFromGuest`, `findLeftovers`, `bringDeviceCharts`, `forgetDeviceCharts`.

## Not done yet

- The owner cannot leave or delete a band (there is no ownership transfer). Members can leave.
- Per-person private charts do not exist; everything a person imports goes into the current band.
- Google sign-in uses a popup and falls back to a redirect when the popup is blocked.
